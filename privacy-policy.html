Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PromptVerse (“we”, “our” or “the website”) handles information when you visit promptverse-official.vercel.app. Please read it together with the terms of any third-party services you choose to use.

Information you provide

The information you submit is transmitted to that service so it can deliver your message to us; Formspree may process information under its own privacy terms. Do not submit passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information through the forms.

Built-in text utilities are designed to process the text you enter locally in your browser. Saved prompt bookmarks and certain preferences may be stored in your browser's local storage. Learn more at Google's advertising policies and technologies and Google's Privacy Policy.

How information is used

Information submitted through contact forms is used to respond to requests, handle feedback and maintain the website. Analytics information is used to understand site performance and improve content and navigation. We do not sell personal information submitted directly to us through the contact form.

External links and data retention

Our pages may link to external AI providers or other websites. Their privacy practices are outside our control; review their policies before sharing information.
