Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

At PromptVerse AI (accessible from https://promptverse-official.vercel.app/), protecting the privacy of our visitors is one of our primary priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by PromptVerse AI and how we use it.

1. Client-Side Browser Utilities (Zero Data Storage)

All interactive web utility tools on PromptVerse AI (including the Word Counter, Text Case Converter, Extra Space Remover, Text Repeater, and AI Token & Cost Estimator) run 100% locally inside your web browser using JavaScript. We do not transmit, log, or store any text or prompts you paste into these tools on our servers.

2. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you use our Contact Us form or Submit a Prompt form (powered by Formspree), we collect your name, email address, and message content solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry or reviewing your submitted AI prompt.

3. Log Files & Google Analytics

PromptVerse AI uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate visitor traffic, popular pages, and device types. This data may include anonymized IP addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring pages. This information is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

4. Cookies and Google AdSense Advertising

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to PromptVerse AI and other sites on the internet. Visitors may opt out of the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.

5. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA regulations, users have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information submitted via our contact forms. To exercise these rights, please contact us directly.
