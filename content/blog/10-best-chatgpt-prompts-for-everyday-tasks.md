Useful prompts explain what you need, provide context and describe the answer you want. These templates are starting points: personalize the bracketed details for your situation.

## 1. Plan your day

Act as a practical planning assistant. My tasks are [list], available time is [hours], and priorities are [priorities]. Create a realistic schedule with breaks, identify the three most important tasks and flag anything that may not fit.

## 2. Write a professional email

Draft a [tone] email to [recipient] about [topic]. Include [key points]. Keep it [length]. Do not invent dates or promises. Provide a subject line and polite closing.

## 3. Understand a difficult topic

Teach me [topic] as a beginner. Explain the core idea in plain language, use an analogy, show an example and finish with five short questions to check my understanding.

## 4. Improve a paragraph

Edit the text below for clarity, grammar and flow while preserving its meaning and voice. Show the polished version first, then list the main changes: [paste text].

## 5. Prepare for an interview

Act as an interviewer for a [job title] role in [industry]. Ask one question at a time, wait for my answer, then give constructive feedback based on the experience I provide.

## 6. Compare options

Compare [A] and [B] for [use case]. Evaluate cost, benefits, limitations, learning curve and long-term fit. Separate known facts from assumptions.

## 7. Create a study plan

Build a [number]-week learning plan for [subject]. My level is [level] and I can study [time] daily. Include goals, practice, revision and checkpoints.

## 8. Brainstorm content ideas

Generate 15 original ideas about [topic] for [audience] on [platform]. Include a hook, key takeaway and suggested format. Avoid repetitive angles.

## 9. Break down a project

Turn [goal] into milestones, tasks, dependencies, effort estimates and risks. Start with the smallest useful next step and list decisions I need to make.

## 10. Plan a meal

Suggest a meal using [ingredients]. Respect [dietary requirements]. Give quantities, preparation steps, cooking time and substitutions. Ask before assuming ingredients not listed.

## Get better results

Add context that changes the answer: goal, audience, constraints, skill level and available resources. Request a format such as a checklist or step-by-step plan. Treat the first response as a draft and give clear revision feedback.

## Key takeaway

Save the templates that match tasks you repeat, then personalize the bracketed details. Better context and a clear output format usually matter more than making a prompt longer.
