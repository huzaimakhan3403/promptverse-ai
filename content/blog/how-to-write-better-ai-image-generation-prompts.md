AI image generators respond to descriptions of what should appear and how it should look. Clear visual details guide the result, although every model interprets prompts differently.

## Start with the main subject

Describe the central subject first. Include defining characteristics, action, setting and details that must remain visible. Replace broad phrases with concrete visual information.

## Describe composition and framing

Explain where the subject sits, what appears in the foreground and background, and how much space surrounds it. Useful terms include close-up, wide shot, overhead view and centered composition. Set an aspect ratio when supported.

## Choose lighting and color

Lighting shapes mood. Specify soft window light, golden-hour sunlight, dramatic side lighting or diffused studio light. A restrained palette such as warm earth tones or cool blue-grey can help create consistency.

## Set the visual style

Choose a coherent medium or style: editorial photography, watercolor, 3D product render, minimalist vector art or cinematic concept art. Avoid combining unrelated styles unless contrast is intentional.

## A reusable image prompt formula

**Subject + action + environment + composition + lighting + palette + style + important details.**

Example: A glass greenhouse in a meadow at sunrise, wide cinematic composition, soft golden backlight, dew-covered grass, muted green and amber palette, editorial landscape photography.

## Iterate one change at a time

Generate an initial image, identify the biggest mismatch and change one or two details. If framing is wrong, clarify composition; if mood is wrong, adjust lighting and color.

## Avoid common problems

Conflicting styles, excessive adjectives and too many competing subjects make results less predictable. Put essential details first. Check spelling carefully when generating text inside images.

## Key takeaway

Think like an art director: specify the subject, scene, composition, lighting and style. Then iterate deliberately instead of adding endless keywords.
